Billd

BILLD is a powerful billing and invoicing solution designed to simplify financial operations for modern businesses. It streamlines the entire billing cycle—from invoice creation to payment tracking—ensuring accuracy, speed, and transparency. With automated workflows, real-time insights, and seamless integration capabilities, BILLD helps businesses reduce manual effort, improve cash flow management, and deliver a professional billing experience.

Features

End-to-end marketing & technology solutions for restaurants — five connected modules that cover every role in the house.

Role-Based Admin

Tailored access levels for super admins, store managers, kitchen staff, and billing teams mean every person on the floor sees exactly the tools they need, and nothing they don't.

Table Order App

A simple QR-code scan lets diners browse the menu and place orders directly from the table, cutting wait times and reducing order-taking errors.

Waiter App

A dedicated handheld view keeps waitstaff in sync with the kitchen, so orders get taken, served, and billed without a single ticket getting lost.

Restaurant E-Commerce

A branded online storefront brings the same menu to delivery and takeaway customers, with orders flowing straight into the same billing pipeline.

Kitchen App

Kitchen Order Tickets land on screen the moment they're placed, giving cooks a clear, ordered queue and cutting down on missed or duplicated items.

Business Type

Built to flex across formats — from a single counter to a full-service floor — Billd adapts its billing flow to how each business actually runs.
