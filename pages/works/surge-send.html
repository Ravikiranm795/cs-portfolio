/UI/UX — File-Sharing Web App Design

Surge Send

A fast, simple file-sharing experience built around clarity and speed, helping people send and store large files without friction.

Project Intro

Surge Send needed an identity and interface that made file sharing and storing feel fast, easy, instant, and secure — backed up by a friendly illustration style that keeps a technical product approachable.

Core Features

Five core capabilities anchor the product: Pause/Resume for uninterrupted transfers, Super Surge for guaranteed delivery, Surge On the Go for access from any device, Surge Security for password-protected files, and Surge Track for keeping tabs on every transfer.

Dashboard

A single-screen transfer flow keeps the essentials — files, recipient and sender emails, a personal message, and access controls — within reach of one Transfer button.

Icon Set

A small custom icon set carries the product's visual language from marketing pages down into in-app cards and settings.

Typography & Colours

#1D48E0 #8DD4FF #EDEFFC #7E7F7F

Landing Page

The marketing site walks visitors from a welcoming hero moment through the product's benefits to an FAQ page that anticipates the questions people actually ask.

The Process

An "Easy-Breezy Guide" breaks sending a file down to three moves — drag and drop files, enter email IDs, transfer and let it fly — with a lightweight flow underneath covering personalized messages, expiration dates, download permissions, and Super Surge.

Mailers

A referral email and a welcome email keep the same illustration style and voice, easing new and invited users into the product.
